FUNDAMENTALS

Core Queueing Concepts

  • Arrival Rate (λ): λ = 1 / Mean Arrival Time
  • Service Rate (μ): μ = 1 / Mean Service Time
  • Utilization (ρ): ρ = λ / (c × μ) (Must be < 1.0)
  • Little's Law: L = λ × W & Lq = λ × Wq
  • Total Time: W = Wq + (1 / μ)
SINGLE PUMP (c = 1)

M/M/1 & M/G/1 Formulas

  • M/M/1 (Exponential): Lq = ρ² / (1 - ρ) Empty system chance: P₀ = 1 - ρ
  • M/G/1 (Pollaczek–Khinchine): Lq = (λ²σ² + ρ²) / [2(1 - ρ)] Proves service variance (σ²) directly lengthens the queue.
  • G/G/1 (Sakasegawa Approx): Wq ≈ [ρ / (1 - ρ)] × [(Ca² + Cs²) / 2] × (1/μ)
MULTI PUMP (c ≥ 2)

M/M/c & G/G/c Formulas

  • M/M/c (Erlang-C Delay Formula): P(Wait) = [aᶜ / (c!(1 - ρ))] × P₀ Lq = P(Wait) × ρ / (1 - ρ) Offered load: a = λ / μ
  • G/G/c (Sakasegawa Multi-Server): Wq ≈ [ρ^(√(2(c+1))-1) / (c(1 - ρ))] × [(Ca² + Cs²) / 2] × (1/μ)
